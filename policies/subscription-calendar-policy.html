Subscription Calendar Privacy Policy

Transparency and trust for our users

Last Updated: December 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Subscription Calendar ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This policy applies to information we collect in this App and in email, text, and other electronic communications sent through or in connection with this App.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Most of your data is stored securely in your personal iCloud container, which we cannot access. However, to provide our services, we process the following types of data:

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you use Sign in with Apple, we receive an anonymized User ID and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address. Apple may hide your real email address by providing us with a private relay email address (e.g., dpdcnf87nu@privaterelay.appleid.com).
  • Subscription Data: We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. We collect anonymous App User IDs and purchase history to validate your receipt and unlock premium features.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous data about how you use the app (e.g., button taps, screen views) to improve user experience. This data is not linked to your personal identity.
  • Device Information: We may collect information about your mobile device and internet connection, including the device's unique device identifier, operating system, and mobile network information.

C. Data Stored in iCloud (CloudKit)

Your financial entries, calendar data, and subscription details are stored in your personal iCloud private container.

  • We do not have access to this data.
  • It is encrypted and stored by Apple.
  • It is synced across your devices signed in to the same iCloud account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App.
  • Process your subscription payments and unlock "Pro" features (via RevenueCat).
  • Authenticate you and sync your account status (via Sign in with Apple).
  • Send you local notifications about upcoming bills (these are scheduled locally on your device and are not sent to our servers).

3. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. These services may process data on our behalf:

  • RevenueCat: Used for subscription management and receipt validation.
    • Data Collected: Purchase history, Anonymous App User ID.
    • Privacy Policy: RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple (iCloud & Sign in with Apple): Used for data storage and authentication.
    • Data Collected: Financial data (encrypted in iCloud), Name/Email (authentication).
    • Privacy Policy: Apple Privacy Policy

4. Your Rights (GDPR - European Union)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.

Legal Basis for Processing: We process your personal data based on Contractual Necessity (to provide the App's core services) and Legitimate Interests (to improve the App and prevent fraud).

To exercise these rights, please contact us at mari@visioncore-dynamics.com.

5. Your Rights (CCPA/CPRA - California, USA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you specific rights:

  • Right to Know: You can request to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You can request that we delete any personal information we have collected from you.
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell your personal information. Therefore, we do not offer an "Opt-Out of Sale" mechanism.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

Categories of Information Collected (Last 12 Months):

  • Identifiers: User ID, Email address (if provided via Sign in with Apple).
  • Commercial Information: Records of products or services purchased (Subscription status).

To exercise these rights, please contact us at mari@visioncore-dynamics.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

  • CloudKit Data: Retained as long as you keep it in your iCloud account. You can delete it at any time by deleting the App or managing your iCloud storage.
  • Subscription Data: Retained by RevenueCat to validate your "Lifetime" or "Subscription" status.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, complying with GDPR or CCPA, please contact us:
